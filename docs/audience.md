# Target Audience and Value Proposition

> **Status:** Documented for the promotion phase (live site, growing audience).
> **Last updated:** 2026-09-02
> **Issue:** [#74](https://github.com/zedxter/pizdato/issues/74)

## Target Audience

### Primary audience: Russian-speaking internet audience, 25–40

The primary target audience is Russian-speaking internet users aged 25–40 who are
active on Telegram, follow internet culture and memes, and appreciate dark humor,
linguistic play, and ironic self-reflection.

**Characteristics:**

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Language | Native Russian speakers, including diaspora (RU, UA, BY, KZ, DE, IL, US) |
| Age | 25–40 (core), with secondary reach 18–24 and 40–50 |
| Platform | Active Telegram users; familiar with channel culture, cross-posts, polls |
| Internet culture | Follows memes, language trends, absurd news, irony as a communication mode |
| Values | Humor over moralizing, skepticism toward hype, appreciation of linguistic play |
| Reading habits | Consumes long-form articles on language/culture topics via Yandex or Telegram |
| Tech literacy | Comfortable with web apps, no registration required = friction-reduced |
| Content preference | Mixed short-form (TG posts, polls) and medium-form (articles, essays) |

### Secondary audiences

| Segment | Description | Relevance |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Russian-language content creators | Bloggers, journalists, writers exploring language phenomena | Source of organic mentions and backlinks |
| Internet linguists / philologists | Audiences interested in Russian slang, etymology, cultural shifts | Organic search traffic from topic magnets |
| Telegram channel owners (adjacent niches) | Humor, absurd news, language, culture channels — 500–5k subs | Cross-promotion partners |
| English-speaking curiosity visitors | Linguists, translators, culture watchers who encounter the site via MCP or English-language references | Incidental, not targeted |

### Audience discovery path

Typical user journeys to pizdato.net:

1. **Search-driven** — lands on a magnet article (e.g. "синдром отложенной жизни") via Yandex, reads the content, clicks the vote CTA or the Telegram link
2. **Telegram-driven** — discovers the channel via cross-promotion, sees a daily stats post or a news verdict, follows to the site
3. **Word-of-mouth** — receives a share link ("Кинь другу") or a wisdom quote screenshot from someone who already voted
4. **Direct curiosity** — types "пиздато" into search after hearing the word in conversation; finds the FAQ/Pizdato page

The current phase favours path 1 (SEO content magnets) and path 2 (TG cross-promotion).

## Value Proposition

### Core value proposition

> **One honest click to push humanity's score — without registration, without scales, without performance.**

Pizdato.net reduces the complex evaluation of "how things are going" to a single
binary gesture: **пиздато** or **хуёво**. It is a collective mood ring for the
Russian-speaking internet, expressed in the most native vocabulary possible.

### Why this matters

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Social media feeds are curated performances — everyone shows a highlight reel | One anonymous vote per person; no profiles, no branding, no followers |
| Opinion polls are bureaucratic, multi-scale, and rarely honest | Binary choice with zero friction; one click, done |
| News creates a skewed negative picture of the world | The collective score gives people a chance to push back with their own assessment |
| Russian-language internet lacks a lightweight, ironic "barometer" site | Native vocabulary (пиздато/хуёво), cultural references, no moralising |

### Key differentiators

1. **One vote forever.** No farming, no multi-account advantage, no polling fatigue. The vote is a gesture, not a metric.
2. **No registration.** No email, no login, no captcha. A cookie session and IP-level rate-limiting is the only enforcement.
3. **Language-first brand.** The word "пиздато" is the brand — it already lives in Russian spoken culture. The site rides existing linguistic recognition rather than inventing a new label.
4. **Content magnets + vote funnel.** Articles draw organic search traffic on high-interest Russian language topics, then pivot readers to vote and subscribe to the Telegram channel.
5. **News feed as a mirror.** The /lenta page shows how news verdicts have pushed the global score, making the abstract counter tangible.

### What the product is NOT

- Not a social network (no profiles, no comments on votes, no friend graph)
- Not a polling platform (no custom polls, no tournament brackets)
- Not a serious analytics tool (the score is a collective gesture, not a research instrument)
- Not a monetised product (promotion phase — audience first, revenue is out of scope)

### Value for each audience segment

| Segment | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Casual voter (core) | Quick catharsis: one click registers your take on the world; shared wisdom quote makes it shareable |
| Content reader | In-depth articles on language/culture with a satisfying pivot to action (vote + subscribe) |
| Telegram subscriber | Daily stats, news verdicts, wisdom — a low-friction ritual that builds the habit of checking in |
| Cross-promotion partner | Audiences that are topic-adjacent, engaged, and convert well — a good swap target |
| Search engine | Fresh, original, non-generative content about language with clear topical authority signals |
