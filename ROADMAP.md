## Guiding principles

1. **Content first** — the site is a funnel for the channel; the channel is the product.
2. **No paid acquisition** — organic growth only (viral, SEO, cross-posting).
3. **Playbook over scale** — pizdato is a promo polygon; learnings go to commercial projects.
4. **Fewer, stronger pages** — over thin content farms.
5. **Self-hosted infra** — no cloud vendor lock-in, one VPS for everything.
