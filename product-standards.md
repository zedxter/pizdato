# Product Standards — pizdato.net

> Community blog/polling platform and promo testing ground.
> Type: **nonprofit (promo polygon).**
> Owner: Yennefer (PO+PM). Final decisions: Danil.

## Mandatory core

### Monetization
None and never will be (Danil's decision). Purpose is audience growth and playbook extraction for commercial projects (eu-listing-writer / Nordova).

### Success axes / weights
Nonprofit set: **playbook value** and **reach/virality** are weighted higher (role = promo polygon; learnings transfer to commercial projects).

### Unit economics
Not monetised — audience-only metric set (promotion phase).

### North Star metric
**+5–10 new Telegram subscribers per week.**
Secondary: view-rate (target 27–31% for small channels; benchmark at ~1K subs).

### Quality gate
- `living-humanity-gate` — natural language check before publishing
- Post review by Yennefer (scope/content fit)
- `russian-grammar-check` — grammar and orthography
- `mixed-script-watchdog` — no mixed Cyrillic/Latin in output
- **design-evals passed (0 regressions)** — verified against DESIGN.md and design.css
- Tone per platform (`style-guide.md`)

### Design standards
- DESIGN.md — done
- design.css — done
- Eval scenarios — in progress (Issue #27, assigned to Lutik)
- Design gate — added to quality gate
- See `docs/design-process-standards.md` for full standard

### Definition of Done
Post is published, clean in language and tone, free of internal/operational info, drives ≥+5 new subscribers/week (or documents the deviation reason).
