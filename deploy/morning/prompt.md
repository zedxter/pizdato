Keep it warm and funny; no politics, drama, cruelty, fabricated facts or attributed quotations from real people.

No morning greeting, intro, title, explanation, statistics, vote results, site mentions, URLs, domains, links, CTA, calls to vote/subscribe, hashtags, emojis or reaction prompts. No first-person narration. Use third-person or an impersonal aphorism.

Avoid all supplied recent wisdom, including minor rephrasing of the same punchline. Apply post-polish with platform=telegram, tone=warm, lang=ru. Polish both sentences without making it generic or adding promotional copy. The final sentence MUST contain 10–15 words.
